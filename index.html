Hi, I’m Eashan, a full-stack developer with a Computer Science and Data Science degree from Rutgers. I’ve built mobile apps, web apps, AI agents and machine learning projects at Rutgers, Voya Financial and on my own. My iOS app ScarletParking hit 1,500+ downloads and Apple’s Top 100 in Navigation, and I built RU Subletting, a subletting marketplace for college students. I also developed Tandem, an agentic coordinator that lets AI coding agents on separate machines collaborate. What I enjoy most is shipping things that solve a problem someone really has. It started in a high school app development class, when I built little games and utility apps and figured out that a laptop and an idea were enough to make something real.

I’ve been a gamer my whole life, so every project feels like a quest I need to finish (which is why this site is game themed).
